# Curiosities

1. **Four missing movies.** The executable names twelve THP pairs; the disc
   has eight. `0_00_1`, `7_07_1`, `7_08_1` and `8_13_1` (each `n` and `w`)
   are referenced but absent: cut, or replaced by real-time scenes.

2. **Two Englishes.** The PAL disc carries `_us` and `_gb` packs side by
   side, 246 files each: the American localisation and a British one.

3. **Debug mini-games in the retail pack.** `fpack/mini/cmn/8000.fpk`
   holds `mini_debug_darts.seq`, `mini_debug_slash.seq` and
   `mini_debug_stab.seq` next to the real mini-games: test beds for the
   sword gestures, left in. Reachable? Useful as a lab for the mouse
   scheme if they are.

4. **Special moves shout in Japanese romaji**: the streams are named
   `senkourekkazuki` (a flash-strike), `syakunetsu` (scorching), `zettai`
   (absolute), `toudo`, `zigo`, with a take number and a `_32` suffix.

5. **Every movie twice**, 4:3 and 16:9, a pair of files each: in 2007 the
   game already did widescreen properly, not by stretching.
